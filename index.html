About Pipe Pilot

A practical platform for piping work.

Pipe Pilot is being built to make professional isometric drawing faster and easier in the field—giving piping professionals a practical way to create CAD-quality drawings directly from a phone or tablet, without the complexity of traditional CAD software.

01

Isometric drawing

Build piping runs using directional input, measured segments and fitting-aware connections on an isometric workspace.

02

Fitting intelligence

Apply fitting rules, rotation, branching and connection logic to reduce guesswork as a drawing develops.

03

Field references

Keep fitting dimensions, pipe data, torque values and layout tools accessible from the same platform.

Privacy

Clear and limited data collection.

Privacy summary

Pipe Pilot collects information that visitors voluntarily provide, including email addresses submitted through the MailerLite signup form and information sent directly by email.

MailerLite is used to collect subscriptions, send email updates and provide form and email engagement analytics. Pipe Pilot does not currently use separate website analytics, advertising trackers or advertising cookies.

Privacy requests, including access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent, may be sent to craig@pipepilotapp.com.
