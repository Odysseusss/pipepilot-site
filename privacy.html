Privacy

Website privacy summary.

This summary covers pipepilotapp.com and the public demo entry. Account and application services may provide additional notices at the point of collection.

Information you provide

Pipe Pilot receives information visitors choose to provide, including email addresses submitted for updates or account access and information sent directly by email. MailerLite is used for optional website update subscriptions and related delivery and engagement records.

Live demo

The logged-out demo does not create a guest account and does not save a drawing to the server. Demo drawing state is ephemeral and is intended to disappear when the browser session is refreshed or closed. If a visitor elects to create an account during the same active demo session, the app may keep the in-memory drawing available so work can continue after authentication.

Advertising and service providers

The public website may display advertising after the site has been reviewed and approved by the advertising provider. Service providers may process limited technical data required to deliver the site, prevent abuse or measure their own service operation. Pipe Pilot does not sell personal information.

Your choices

Update emails are optional. Privacy requests—including access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent—may be sent to craig@pipepilotapp.com.
