Product changelog

What changed—and why.

Pipe Pilot is actively developed from field tests. This log highlights product behavior that materially changes drawing or fabrication workflows.

October 2026 — voice routes and view geometry

  • Added ordered multi-leg voice routes so one instruction can create connected measured legs.
  • Made flange gasket treatment and thickness first-class joint data instead of a dropped voice modifier.
  • Added four fixed drawing viewpoints with view-aware compass projection.
  • Unified projected fitting outlets and child-run starts to prevent rotated-view detachment.
  • Suppressed construction hatching when a plane or projected triangle is edge-on.
  • Added a visible upload, transcription and interpretation state to remove silent voice-processing gaps.

September 2026 — deterministic command normalization

  • Expanded the fitting vocabulary and normalized spoken fitting aliases to catalog IDs.
  • Separated fitting-angle numbers from measured-length numbers.
  • Changed clarification handling so the latest answer supersedes the unresolved slot instead of creating a loop.

August 2026 — drawing communication

  • Added heat mapping and category callouts.
  • Improved adjustable dimension placement and measured-run labels.
  • Added rolled-offset construction geometry and itemized drawing callouts.

Detailed August release captures remain available in the August 2026 update.
